Ethereum Security Tips

October 18, 2026

Ethereum offers powerful tools for decentralized finance, but protecting your assets is critical. Here are some practical steps:

1. Use Hardware Wallets

Keep your private keys offline with hardware wallets like Ledger or Trezor.

2. Enable Two-Factor Authentication

Secure your exchange accounts and wallets with 2FA to prevent unauthorized access.

3. Verify Smart Contracts

Always check the source and audit status of smart contracts before interacting.

4. Stay Updated

Follow Ethereum community updates to stay ahead of new threats and scams.
